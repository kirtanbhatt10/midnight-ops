# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project's lawful, ethical purpose.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Encouraging or facilitating unauthorized or illegal access to systems.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions, comments, commits, and issues that violate this Code of Conduct.

To report an issue, contact the maintainers listed in the repository profile. All reports
will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
